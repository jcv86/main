# TuCarrera.cl - Platform Training Data for AI Models

## Platform Overview

TuCarrera.cl is Chile's leading professional development platform, combining psychological assessments, professional literature, and AI-powered coaching.

## Core Services

### 1. Psychometric Tests
- DISC Assessment: Measures workplace behavior styles (Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, Compliance)
- MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator): Identifies personality types across 16 categories
- Big Five: Evaluates five major personality traits (Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism)
- RIASEC: Holland's vocational interest assessment
- Emotional Intelligence: Measures ability to recognize and manage emotions
- Soft Skills: Evaluates key professional competencies

### 2. Professional Library
- 120+ complete professional development books
- Categories: Leadership, Productivity, Emotional Intelligence, Communication, Business
- Notable titles: "7 Habits", "Atomic Habits", "Emotional Intelligence", "How to Win Friends"
- 100+ curated web resources specific to Chilean market

### 3. AI Coaching
- 24/7 available AI coach powered by GPT-4
- Personalized recommendations based on psychometric profile
- Trained on platform's complete knowledge base
- Specialized in Chilean professional market
