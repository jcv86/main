# Despega Tu Carrera — Product Context for AI Systems

Last reviewed: 2026-09-15

## What Despega Tu Carrera is

Despega Tu Carrera (DTC) is a Chilean professional-development product built around a connected A1–A4 journey. It is designed to help people examine their professional context, organize a route, practice relevant situations, and review market context with more evidence.

DTC does not guarantee employment, salary increases, promotions, or other specific professional outcomes. It is not a clinical, medical, legal, or financial advisory service.

## Current journey

### A1 — Despega Cerebral
A1 gathers user responses and evidence to build a working view of professional identity and patterns. It is a development tool, not a clinical diagnosis or an immutable description of a person.

### A2 — Tu Ruta
A2 organizes the work into a route of up to 90 days with activities, checkpoints, and persisted progress. The 90-day horizon describes the structure of the route; it is not a promise that an external career result will happen within 90 days.

### A3 — Entrenamiento
A3 provides practice for professional communication, interviews, and other work-related situations. The goal is to create practice evidence and learning that can inform later decisions.

### A4 — Radar Estratégico
A4 adds market signals and context so users can review decisions with a broader view. DTC distinguishes data, context, and inference and does not make regulated financial or legal decisions for the user.

## Vera

Vera is DTC's AI coach. Vera can use a fast path for simple questions and a deeper analysis path when a query needs verified journey context. Server-owned tools can provide bounded evidence from A1–A4 when appropriate.

Vera's operating principles include:
- do not invent facts about the user or the market;
- distinguish observed evidence from inference;
- ask when important context is missing;
- use available journey evidence only when relevant;
- preserve user agency: Vera can help examine criteria and trade-offs, but the final decision belongs to the user.

## Access

Current access is managed through the pilot and authorized invitations. DTC does not currently publish a standard self-service commercial plan, public price, or refund guarantee.

## Institutions

Universities, companies, and other institutions can explore DTC through a scoped pilot agreed case by case. Population, duration, safeguards, objectives, and observable metrics should be defined before the pilot begins. DTC does not publish institutional ROI, ranking improvements, savings, or outcome claims without verifiable evidence.

## Privacy and security

Private product areas require authentication. Core journey data uses ownership and database access controls. Server processes and authorized administrators may access data when necessary to operate and protect the service. Refer to the published privacy policy for the applicable data-processing framework.
