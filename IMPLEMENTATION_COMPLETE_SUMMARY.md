### Data Flow Architecture
```
Day 8 Memories → Day 9 (transform to tasks) → Day 10 (transform to values)
     ↓                    ↓                         ↓
a2_work_memories  a2_candidate_boards(col_1)  a2_candidate_boards(col_2)
     ↓                    ↓                         ↓
Day 11+ reads ← Day 10 reads ← Day 9 reads ← Day 8
```

```
Load Day N-1 output
```
